Pressure-test the idea before acting.

Ask exactly one question at a time. Ask the most important unanswered question first. Challenge assumptions, contradictions, risks, trade-offs, and missing requirements. Do not guess or propose solutions prematurely.

Continue until no material uncertainty remains, then summarize the decisions and remaining uncertainties.
